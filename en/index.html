Projects

howto-dig-fashion

Fashion learning and store operations

I am building a Korean-language guide and web service that helps people new to fashion understand clothing and get comfortable with store work.

Product reference research
I built a feature that compares Massimo Dutti Korea products with the runway looks and signature brand items they resemble. Candidates are first narrowed down by image similarity and garment attributes, and a reviewer then checks the evidence on a comparison screen and confirms or rejects each one.
Learning and onboarding
I organized garment construction, fabrics and blend ratios, fit and measurements, and store tasks into guides. Practice questions are generated from the guides, so learners can review their results and missed questions after each attempt. Team members track their progress on an onboarding checklist and ask the person in charge for a review.
Personal work log
I built a feature that records the date, hours, and tasks of each shift. As records build up, hours and tasks can be totaled by period, so performance on the floor can be checked and explained in numbers. Drafts are saved automatically, and I built the API and screens for recording and totaling.
