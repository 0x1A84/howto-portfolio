## Featured project · howto-dig-fashion

A web application and Korean-language learning library for people studying fashion and preparing for store work.

- **Fashion learning** covers garment construction, fabrics, fit, measurements, and store operations. The application supports timed assessments, answer explanations, and AI-generated practice questions grounded in the guides.
- **Product research** helps reviewers compare Massimo Dutti Korea products with runway looks and signature pieces. It ranks candidates using image similarity and garment attributes, then lets reviewers confirm or reject the suggested matches.
- **Store operations** include branch-scoped member directories and weekly schedules. I implemented schedule authoring and publishing, autosave with conflict handling, and views where staff can see their published schedule and next shift.
- **Technical documentation** accompanies the implementation. I maintain feature specifications, screen definitions, use cases, and database diagrams for workflows such as branch membership and scheduling.
